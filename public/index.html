Getting started

Up and running in 3 steps

No app to download. Just Telegram and optionally a Strava account.

1

Open the bot

Search for @isparo_bot on Telegram and send /start. Sparrow will greet you and ask about your fitness background.

2

Connect Strava

Link your Strava account so Sparrow can import your activity history. Your last 90 days of workouts are used to build a personalised plan. Don't have Strava? You can skip this step.

3

Get your plan

Sparrow generates your first weekly workout plan instantly. Every Sunday evening you'll receive a fresh plan, and every night a reminder for the next day's session.

Reference

Bot commands

Everything you can say to Sparrow.

Command What it does
Plans
/plan Show your current week's workout plan with each day's session, duration, and coaching notes.
/newplan Generate a fresh plan starting from today, syncing your latest Strava activities first.
/checkplan Compare this week's planned workouts against what you actually did. Regenerates the plan only if there's significant divergence (2+ missed or mismatched days).
Exercise Guide
/howto <exercise> Get a concise form guide for any exercise — setup, key cues, and common mistakes — plus a YouTube link. Example: /howto Romanian deadlift
Profile
/profile View your fitness level, goals, training days, and saved context notes.
/update profile Update your fitness level, goals, days/week, limitations, and targets. Fresh input replaces the old values.
Settings
/connect Connect or reconnect your Strava account. Use this if you skipped Strava during onboarding or your connection has expired.
/llm Check which AI model is powering your coach.
/llm gemini Switch to Gemini 2.5 Flash — free, no API key needed. Free
/llm claude <api_key> Switch to Claude Haiku (Anthropic). Requires your own Anthropic API key. See the Models section.
/llm openai <api_key> Switch to GPT-4o mini (OpenAI). Requires your own OpenAI API key. See the Models section.
/reset Clear your profile and restart onboarding from scratch. Useful if your fitness level or goals have changed significantly.
/start Begin onboarding (new users) or get a welcome-back summary with all commands (existing users).
/help Show all available commands inside Telegram.
Anything else you type is treated as a question to your AI coach — ask about your training load, recovery, technique, nutrition, or anything fitness related.
AI Models

Choose your AI coach

Sparrow works out of the box with Gemini (free). Switch to Claude or OpenAI if you want more detailed, nuanced responses and are happy to use your own API key.

Default · Free
✦

Gemini 2.5 Flash

Google — no API key needed

  • Free with no billing setup
  • Fast response times
  • Good for everyday coaching questions
How to activate Gemini is already active by default. To switch back after trying another model:
/llm gemini
Paid · Your key
◆

Claude Haiku

Anthropic — detailed, well-structured responses

  • More detailed coaching explanations
  • Better structured weekly plans
  • Strong reasoning about training load
Setup 1. Go to console.anthropic.com
2. Create an account and add billing credits
3. Go to API Keys → Create Key
4. Copy your key (starts with sk-ant-)
5. In Telegram, send:
/llm claude sk-ant-YOUR_KEY_HERE

⚠ Delete the message after sending to protect your key.
Paid · Your key
⬡

GPT-4o mini

OpenAI — fast and capable

  • Concise, practical coaching advice
  • Widely used and well-tested
  • Cost-effective among paid models
Setup 1. Go to platform.openai.com/api-keys
2. Create an account and add billing credits
3. Click Create new secret key
4. Copy your key (starts with sk-)
5. In Telegram, send:
/llm openai sk-YOUR_KEY_HERE

⚠ Delete the message after sending to protect your key.
